Who are we?

Founded in 2015 in the Nador region, Thimdrine brings together 25 women who harvest, process, and package their products themselves.

Our beehives are located in the wild thyme hills of the Rif, our olive trees grow on family terraces, and our prickly pears are hand-picked every summer.
